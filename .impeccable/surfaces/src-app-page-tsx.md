# Surface brief: homepage and /work

Scope: `src/app/page.tsx` (mode: Persuade) and `src/app/work/page.tsx` (mode: Read with Experience overlays). Direction is pinned by `req.md`; no concept roll was run.

Audience and job: clients decide to get in touch; hiring teams find work, experience, skills, resume. Primary actions: "Explore My Work" and "Let's Talk".

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Swiss editorial specimen for an engineer. Type and structure do the work a portfolio usually hands to screenshots and skill badges. It refuses the developer-template arrangement of avatar, badge cloud, and card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground (#080808), white ink, zinc grey secondary, 1px #292929 hairlines, square corners. Geist for display and text, Geist Mono only for data labels, Bodoni Moda italic for selected statements. Inverting rows (#EDEDED on hover). Wireframe line drawings as the only imagery.

STORY: The visitor sees who this is and what he builds, sees four real projects with honest labels, sees six capabilities, then contacts him or opens a case study.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Headline "Engineering ideas into meaningful digital products." at up to 7rem, left-aligned on a 12-column grid, over a full-bleed WebGL wireframe lattice with particles weighted to the right. Supporting paragraph and two actions under it. A hairline status rail along the bottom carries location and availability.

FORM: Brief-pinned monochrome Swiss editorial. Signature interaction: the lattice turns toward the cursor, and rows invert under it. Motion grammar: masked line reveals on headings, scrubbed parallax on diagrams, one slow ambient rotation. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
